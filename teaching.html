Teaching Assistant and Lab Instructor · 30 students

An introduction to geomorphology. Students learned river, glacial, hillslope, and wind-driven processes and the landforms they produce.

Teaching Assistant · 15 students

A survey of biogeochemical interactions among Earth’s crust, oceans, and atmosphere, with attention to carbon, nitrogen, phosphorus, and sulfur cycles and human perturbations.
