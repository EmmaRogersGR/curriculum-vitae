Current projects

Sep 2022 – present

Basaltic clast transport and abrasion

PhD Candidate with Dr. Marisa Palucis

Palucis Planetary Geomorphology Research Group, Dartmouth College

Rounded basaltic pebbles on Mars record how far, how fast, and in what conditions they were carried by water. I combine laboratory experiments with field campaigns at terrestrial analog environments to understand how basalt abrades during transport. These results help decipher past fluvial conditions and the prevailing climate on Mars at the time of deposition.

Ongoing

Paleoclimate of the Northern Atacama Desert

Lead, with Dr. Marisa Palucis, Dr. Alexander Morgan, and Lily Tuttle

Dartmouth College

Dating alluvial fan stratigraphy with luminescence and radiocarbon to constrain how climate changed in the Northern Atacama Desert, a Martian analog. The work uses stratigraphic sections, geochronology samples, fan mapping, and UAV imagery from field campaigns in Chile.

Previous research

May 2020 – Dec 2021

Hydrated silica in Melas Chasma, Mars

Undergraduate Researcher with Dr. Briony Horgan

Planetary Surface Processes Research Group, Purdue University

Evaluated the mineralogy and morphology of hydrated silica in Melas Chasma, Valles Marineris. The work gives insight into hydrothermal and lacustrine environments on Mars, with implications for astrobiology.

Feb 2019 – Dec 2021

Pleistocene Lake Tight

Undergraduate Researcher with Dr. Darryl Granger

Darryl Granger Research Group, Purdue University

Mapped lake sediments of Lake Tight, a Pleistocene proglacial lake in present-day Ohio, Kentucky, and West Virginia, to date it and calculate its elevation. Field observations of surface sediments and cave deposits show evidence of crustal flexure beneath glacial ice.

Jun – Aug 2020

Equatorial gullies on Mars

Planetary Research Intern with Dr. Virginia Gulick

SETI Institute REU

Used hydrologic models and digital terrain models to estimate water discharge, volume, and erosion rates of martian equatorial gully systems, with implications for paleoclimate change.

Jan – May 2021

Ice sample return mission concept

Undergraduate Science Advisor

Ali Bramson Research Group, Purdue University

Worked with an interdisciplinary team of engineering and science undergraduates on a hypothetical ice sample return mission for the AIAA Space Design Competition 2021, covering science goals, technology needs, and cost estimates.
