Nuuk and Kangerlussuaq · 2 weeks

Teaching Assistant

Western U.S. and Canada · 4 weeks

Dec 2024; Jan 2023

Atacama Desert, Chile

Pica and San Pedro de Atacama · 2 weeks in 2024 (lead); Pica, 1.5 weeks in 2023

Measured mudflow depths, compiled stratigraphic sections, collected ground-penetrating radar profiles, and mapped alluvial fan morphology to infer depositional processes and post-glacial environmental change, with comparisons to Mars. Also collected UAV imagery of salars, luminescence geochronology samples, and stratigraphic sections to constrain the paleoclimate and developing hyperaridity of the Northeastern Atacama.

Jul 2024

Iceland

Þingvellir (lead) and Höfn · 2 weeks

Cold and icy Martian analog. On the Þórisjökull alluvial plain I used Wolman pebble counts, GPR transects, infiltrometers, and eBee drone imagery to measure sediment flux and basalt abrasion. Near Höfn and in the Fjallabak Nature Preserve I assisted a Smithsonian National Air and Space Museum team collecting samples for VNIR spectroscopy of chemical alteration on alluvial fans.

Feb 2023; Mar 2024

Mojave Desert, California and Nevada

Primm · 1 week each trip (lead)

Arid Martian analog. Led expeditions doing pebble counts, GPS cross-sectional transects, and UAV imagery to quantify abrasion on granitic and basaltic fans, for comparison with laboratory abrasion experiments.

Oct – Dec 2023

Svalbard

UNIS course AG-826, Arctic Quaternary Environments

Longyearbyen · 6 weeks

Field trips around Longyearbyen to study indicators of past ice sheets, including raised beaches, moraines, and varves.

Jun – Jul 2021

Wasatch-Uinta Field Camp

Park City, Utah · 6 weeks

Field course with nine projects: stratigraphic sections, rock descriptions, regional geologic maps, and bedrock cross sections.
